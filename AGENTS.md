# Agent notes

Cluster use: read `clusterConfigs/msClusterUserGuide.md` before logging in, choosing a partition, submitting a job, or placing data. It is the operational reference for mscluster (login hosts, login-node rules, Slurm, storage, software), based on a live survey of the rebuilt cluster on 10 November 2026. It replaces the 2024 community guide PDF, which is out of date and no longer kept in this repo.
