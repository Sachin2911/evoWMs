## Papers that may already answer the central question

None found.

Nearest, and what each lacks:

- Behavioral Steering of Vision-Language-Action Models in the Low-Parameter Regime (theme 5): compares CMA-ES with policy-gradient RL on 8 to 384 TinyLoRA parameters of a frozen policy; it steers behaviour style, not safety, and the policy is model-free, not a world-model agent.
- Warp: Structured Output-Space Adaptation for Frozen Robot Policies (theme 5): an evolution-strategy adapter study on frozen robot policies under deployment shift; it has no safety constraint.
- SLowRL: Safe Low-Rank Adaptation Reinforcement Learning for Locomotion (theme 5): low-rank adaptation of a pretrained locomotion policy with a recovery policy enforcing safety during fine-tuning; it is gradient-based, not evolved, and its baseline is PPO fine-tuning, not a safety-retrained agent.
- Latent Q-Barrier Shielding for Safe In-Context Reinforcement Learning (theme 3): a shield (context encoder, latent dynamics, ensemble cost critic) fitted before deployment around a frozen in-context RL policy and compared with a safe in-context RL baseline; the frozen policy is already safety-pretrained and is not a world-model agent.
- Generalizing Safety Beyond Collision-Avoidance via Latent-Space Reachability Analysis (theme 3; likewise the Seo et al. and AnySafe latent safety filters in the same theme): safety filters trained in the latent space of a world model and wrapped around a task policy; they are trained by reinforcement learning in imagination, not evolved.
- Safe Reinforcement Learning through Meta-learned Instincts (theme 4): an evolved instinct network that stays fixed during the agent's lifetime modulates the output of an RL policy to avoid hazards; the policy keeps learning and is not frozen, and there is no world model or comparison with a retrained agent.
